📧 Gmail — Requires 2-Step Verification

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/security → turn on 2-Step Verification
  2. Then visit myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
  3. App: Mail · Device: Other · Name: NexusBank → Generate
  4. Copy the 16-character password and paste it below
⚠ If you see "Setting not available for your account" — your account is managed by a school/organization. Use Outlook or Mailtrap instead.

💙 Outlook / Hotmail — Easiest Option!

  1. Use your full Outlook email: yourname@outlook.com or @hotmail.com
  2. Enter your regular Outlook password — no app password needed in most cases
  3. If it fails: go to Microsoft Account Security → App passwords → Create one
✅ Recommended! Works with regular password — no 2FA required for most accounts.

🟣 Yahoo Mail

  1. Go to Yahoo Account Security → enable 2-Step Verification
  2. Generate an App Password for Other App
  3. Use your Yahoo email + the generated app password below

🧪 Mailtrap — Best for Testing (100% Free, No Setup Hassle)

  1. Go to mailtrap.io → sign up free (takes 30 seconds)
  2. Dashboard → Email Testing → Inboxes → click your inbox
  3. Go to SMTP Settings tab → copy Username and Password
  4. Paste them below — all emails go to your Mailtrap inbox (safe, not real customers)
✅ Perfect for student projects! Catches all emails in a dashboard — zero risk of spamming real users.
